# Asset provenance and licences

## Original buttersalt artwork

The custom wordmark paths, seed mark, popcorn outlines, label layouts, colour system, favicon and social layout were created specifically for this project. No stock logo, trademark artwork, paid font, clip art or third-party packaging artwork was copied. SVG logo masters contain editable geometry rather than embedded raster lettering. The social-card raster uses the platform's sans-serif renderer for the accompanying tagline; the distributed brand fonts below are used in the storefront.

**DM Sans** is redistributed under SIL Open Font License 1.1. Source: [Google Fonts DM Sans repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans). Licence: public/fonts/DMSans-OFL.txt. Both font licences are included unmodified. Do not sell either font by itself or remove its licence. WOFF2 subset source URLs and Unicode ranges are recorded in public/fonts/google-fonts-source.css. Logo letterforms were custom drawn; no font licence is required to edit the SVG wordmark paths.

## Generated photographs

The sixteen flavour images were generated with the built-in OpenAI image_gen tool for this project on 14 October 2026, followed by eight edits using the approved wordmark and seed-mark references to refine identity consistency. There are no stock-photo or photographer attributions to invent. Generation and refinement prompts, output filenames and concept status are recorded in docs/image-prompts.json. Original PNG files, pre-refinement bag PNGs and selected refined PNGs are retained in assets/originals/; originals at the tool's default destination were left in place. Exports are resized and encoded with sharp; no ingredient, dietary, nutrition or manufacturing fact should be inferred from them.

These are development concept visualisations. The generated food appearance and packaging should be reviewed against actual approved products before live selling. Merchant approval of final packaging artwork, actual product information and replacement stock photography remains a launch dependency. General hero, family, gifting and lifestyle imagery provenance belongs with those image prompts in the project documentation.
